## Use this card
Status: EDITORIAL_PROPOSAL_NOT_EXECUTED

Candidate behavior: make remembered experience change the next attempt through a testable learning cycle.

When: After an observed success, failure or repeated operator correction suggests a bounded improvement.

### Inputs
- Exact incident/output artifacts and consumer outcome
- One causal hypothesis, baseline, budget and fixed validation criterion

### Procedure
- Hindsight: reconstruct what happened from artifacts and identify uncertainty.
- Insight: propose one causal explanation and a distinguishing observation.
- Validation: prepare a bounded comparison of one intervention against the baseline.
- Evolution: submit the observed result for existing admission; preserve rejected hypotheses and unresolved failures.

### Outputs
- Incident-to-hypothesis record
- Single-intervention comparison plan
- Evidence-bound adoption or rejection proposal

### Failure modes
- Storytelling substituted for causal evidence
- Several changes obscure which helped
- An experiment automatically promoted into policy

### Worked example
- situation: Carriers repeatedly select index-only cards as complete instructions.
- action: Propose a clearer missing-contract cue and compare retrieval decisions under the fixed rubric.
- evidence: Expected: original failures, candidate revision, independent comparison and retained counterexamples.

### Neurosymbolic division of work
- neural: Generate explanations and candidate remedies.
- symbolic: Connect incidents, interventions, comparisons and admission records.

### Evolution contract
Mutable: One declared intervention per comparison
Frozen: Historical evidence and authority boundaries; Baseline, judge and acceptance criteria during comparison
Fitness: Evaluate the targeted failure's recurrence and useful outcomes; recording a lesson does not establish improvement.
