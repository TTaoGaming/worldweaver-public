## Use this card
Status: EDITORIAL_PROPOSAL_NOT_EXECUTED

Candidate behavior: carry unfinished work through interruption without losing identity or repeating uncertain effects.

When: When resuming a bounded task from durable evidence after an attempt ends.

### Inputs
- Original WorkItem, ownership/lease evidence and remaining budget
- Last durable checkpoint, candidate artifacts and effect observations

### Procedure
- Reconstruct completed, pending and uncertain steps from the checkpoint and artifacts.
- Reconcile ownership and uncertain effects through permitted evidence before retrying.
- Choose one continuation step within the original ceiling and remaining budget.
- Return a new checkpoint, exact output references and a bounded next-action handoff.

### Outputs
- Recovery plan tied to the original work identity
- Updated checkpoint candidate and unresolved-effect list

### Failure modes
- A restart creates duplicate ownership
- Unknown effects are repeated blindly
- Retries silently reset the budget

### Worked example
- situation: An extraction attempt ends after saving part of its output.
- action: Compare the saved artifact with required fields and continue only the missing portion under valid ownership.
- evidence: Expected: original identity, prior checkpoint, preserved completed output and independently checked continuation.

### Neurosymbolic division of work
- neural: Diagnose the interruption and select a useful recovery step.
- symbolic: Preserve ownership, checkpoints, effect history and budget accounting.

### Evolution contract
Mutable: Checkpoint clarity and bounded recovery heuristics
Frozen: Work identity, ownership rules and effect ceiling; Retry budget and comparison judge
Fitness: Evaluate recovered useful output, duplicate effects and operator intervention under fixed interruption cases.
