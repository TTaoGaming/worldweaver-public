## Use this card
Status: EDITORIAL_PROPOSAL_NOT_EXECUTED

Candidate behavior: turn the hive's shared purpose into one useful outcome a named consumer can recognize.

When: When choosing the next bounded mission or explaining why existing work matters.

### Inputs
- Current operator intent and source precedence
- Consumer need, acceptance criterion, available effort and effect ceiling

### Procedure
- Describe the beneficiary and observable outcome in one sentence.
- Compare feasible next actions using expected usefulness, effort and unresolved assumptions.
- Select one bounded action and state why it advances the outcome.
- Prepare a WorkItem proposal naming its consumer and completion evidence.

### Outputs
- Purpose-to-outcome explanation
- One reviewable WorkItem proposal with unresolved assumptions

### Failure modes
- Treating agent activity as beneficiary value
- Replacing operator intent with an attractive internal project

### Worked example
- situation: A reader cannot identify which card explains worker continuity.
- action: Propose one navigation improvement and ask the designated evaluator to test the reader's task.
- evidence: Expected: an exact candidate change and an independently observed retrieval outcome.

### Neurosymbolic division of work
- neural: Interpret the need and generate useful alternatives.
- symbolic: Bind intent, consumer, scope and acceptance to explicit records.

### Evolution contract
Mutable: Explanations and action hypotheses
Frozen: Operator intent and effect ceiling; Evaluator and criteria during comparison
Fitness: Compare useful accepted outcomes and operator corrections against the fixed baseline; activity alone earns no credit.
