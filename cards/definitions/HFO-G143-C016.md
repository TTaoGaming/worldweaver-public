## Use this card

Candidate behavior: turn an admitted work packet into one bounded worker attempt with a reviewable result.

When: When preparing a worker for a specific task; recover its admitted ABI before launch.

### Inputs
- WorkItem identity, scope, lease, effect ceiling and budget
- Exact input artifacts, admitted ABI reference, compatible loadout and result contract

### Procedure
- Map supplied inputs and required outputs to the recovered ABI without inventing fields.
- Resolve the carrier and loadout against the task's requirements.
- Prepare an attempt packet; identify missing bindings before execution.
- For an authorized attempt, perform the bounded step and return its candidate artifact plus remaining work.

### Outputs
- Source-bound attempt packet
- Candidate artifact or explicit missing-binding report
- Verification handoff

### Failure modes

- Expanding scope to keep a worker busy
- Reporting dispatch as completion

### Worked example

- action: Bind those inputs, extract only the requested fields, and attach source locations.
