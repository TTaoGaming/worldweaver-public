Candidate behavior: keep the comparison lens still while a candidate changes.

When: Before comparing a baseline with a proposed improvement or reviewing a claimed gain.

### Inputs
- Baseline and candidate revisions
- Fixed task set, budget, evaluator revision and acceptance criteria
- Independently held-out cases and evidence-retention plan

### Procedure
- Record the comparison protocol before inspecting candidate outcomes.
- Keep evaluator access and unseen ground truth separate from candidate authors.
- Evaluate both versions under matched conditions through the authorized evaluator.
- Report outcomes, failures, costs and uncertainty; route evaluator changes to a separate comparison proposal.

### Outputs
- Frozen comparison specification
- Evidence-indexed comparison report or missing-evaluation list

### Failure modes
- Changing criteria after seeing results
- Treating public examples as unseen cases
- Counting self-scored assertions as independent acceptance
