# HFO card art and behavior guide

Status: proposed presentation and evaluation guidance. No images generated. No behavioral trials run. This guide introduces no runtime, ontology replacement, or admission rule.

## Source and creative boundary

Preserve Tao's schema v1.2: HYPERSTITION guides macro PDSA; MYTH supplies a memory palace; ENGINEERING guides micro PDSA through established pattern, origin, and exemplar; CHECKS express invariants; LINKS preserve typed relationships. Source precedence remains Tao, then heritage, then carrier interpretation. Label new visual interpretations as proposals.

Create original HFO editorial science-fantasy: obsidian surfaces, restrained crimson light, ivory highlights, etched technical geometry, tactile mineral and archival-paper detail. Preserve user-authored myth and names. Avoid franchise characters, recognizable franchise framing, copied symbols, and invented claims about Norse deities or historical traditions. Public assets contain no private operational metadata.

## Shared composition

Use a vertical 4:5 art master inside the browser's separate card frame. Place one clear subject in the central 70%, with quiet edge margins for responsive cropping. Establish silhouette first, one secondary relationship second, fine texture last. Use a consistent three-quarter perspective and controlled lighting; avoid busy battle panoramas. Render definitions as conceptual illustrations, without visual trophies implying successful execution.

Generated art contains no lettering, numbers, badges, equations, logos, QR codes, evidence stamps, or UI chrome. Names, types, costs, limits, claims, coordinates, timestamps, source links, CHECKS, and status remain selectable, searchable HTML outside the image. Color inside art carries atmosphere; labeled UI carries operational meaning.

## Type grammar

These are visual treatments of inherited types and relationships, not new classes.

| Type or view | Visual cue |
| --- | --- |
| Land | Wide layered terrain or reservoir; bounded channels suggest capacity. |
| Unit | Compact modular body with visible attachment points; recognizable at thumbnail size. |
| Planeswalker/Apex | Deliberate central presence with an organized constellation; reserve this treatment for inherited Apex typing. |
| Equipment | Detachable object with an exposed coupling; show its attachment relationship. |
| Artifact | Stable object with material specificity: archive, instrument, vessel, or interface. |
| Spell | One directional transformation with legible beginning and end. |
| Instant facet | A single sharp interruption or arriving signal; urgency supplies no authority. |
| Enchantment/aura | A surrounding constraint or field with a visible boundary. |
| Saga/quest | A readable sequence of stations; leave uncompleted stages visually unresolved. |
| Loadout | An arrangement of separate attachments; retain their individual identities. |

Actor, Hluti, seat, carrier, session, and authority remain distinct in UI labels and typed links. Mythic stature does not grant permissions. Avoid depicting loyalty as spendable earned trust. The eight-card hand is a contextual selection; battlefield labels refer to evidenced instances or bindings, with scope and freshness displayed independently of art.

## Accessibility and reading

Use real headings, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, and text labels accompanying icons or color. Target at least 4.5:1 contrast for ordinary UI text and 3:1 for large text and essential controls; verify the implemented palette. Support zoom, reduced motion, and a useful text-only card view.

Alt text describes the image's meaningful subject and relationship in one short sentence. Keep engineering claims and evidence in adjacent text. Use empty alt text for purely decorative duplicates. Example: "An obsidian skald figure surrounded by linked fragments and an open archive." This describes an illustration without claiming autonomy or verification.

Version each accepted prompt and asset against its card revision. Record generator, generation date, source references, and human acceptance separately; do not imply creation grants canonical status.

## Behavioral assay proposal

Freeze tasks, carrier/model versions, tools, budgets, card revisions, and scoring before evaluation. Compare existing instructions alone, factual card text, and the full card presentation. Counterbalance order; reserve unseen cases and ground truth with an independent evaluator. Include missing evidence, stale bindings, incompatible equipment, unavailable capacity, and tempting unauthorized actions.

Score each dimension 0/1/2: carrier/tool fit, authority and budget compliance, evidence discrimination, appropriate HOLD/recovery, and useful task completion. Record artifacts, tokens, time, errors, and false success claims. Compare per-task results against baseline; report failures and uncertainty. Promotion requires predefined acceptance criteria and independent artifact review. Card authors and executing carriers cannot accept their own results. No behavioral improvement is currently established.
